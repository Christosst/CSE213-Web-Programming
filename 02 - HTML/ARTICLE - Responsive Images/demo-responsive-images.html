🎓 Student Takeaway:
  • Use <img srcset="..." sizes="..."> for Resolution Switching (saving bandwidth).
  • Use <picture> for Art Direction (different crops) or Format Switching (AVIF → WebP → JPEG).
  • Always supply width, height, and descriptive alt text on the <img> tag!
