Floats and CSS Positioning Techniques

1. Float: Editorial Text Wrap Only

Float was originally designed in the early days of HTML for newspaper-style editorial text wrapping. Notice how this sentence and paragraph wrap neatly around the bottom and right edges of the floated thumbnail. Instructor takeaway: Remind students never to use floats for general grid or flex layouts!
